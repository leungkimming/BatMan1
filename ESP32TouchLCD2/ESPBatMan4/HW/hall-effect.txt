electric wheelchair battery management by coulomb counting.
components:
- B1:24-29v battery in original wheelchair circuit (do not break warranty)
- B1 29v and GND taken from charging plug
	- R2, R3, C1, R1 divide 29v to within 3.3v and feed to EPS32 IO34 to measure (V)
	- OKI78SR5 regulator to supply stable 5v
- YMKH16 hall-effect measure red cable from battery (contactless measure) 
	- wheelchair at rest is 2.0553v. when moving will drop below 2.0553v and when recharging will climb above 2.0553v.
	- feed to ESP32 IO35 with C1 to measure current (+/- A) Amp in/out
	- C4, Q1 closes and feed GND to TDO (GPIO15 pull high internally) to wake up ESP32 after long time low current hibernation. No need to consider wake up during recharging because I need to unplug and plug in again anyway and this will reboot ESP32.

Vbe @5V 2ma min=0.58 typical=0.63, max=0.7
